# Facts in the example script, with sources

- Tornado winds can pass 200 mph (EF5 on the Enhanced Fujita scale): NOAA / National Weather Service, "The Enhanced Fujita Scale".
- Tornado safe rooms are tested against a 15-pound 2x4 board hitting at 100 mph: FEMA P-361, "Safe Rooms for Tornadoes and Hurricanes".
- Gooseneck (hooked) vents keep rain out of below-ground vents: a common building practice; check your local code.
